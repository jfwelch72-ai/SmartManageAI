# TFR Construction Management Platform

## Overview
The TFR Construction Management Platform is an internal tool for TFR Construction, designed to manage the entire project lifecycle from bid tracking to project completion. It centralizes project data, user management, and daily operations to enhance efficiency, compliance, and oversight. The platform aims to integrate AI for operational intelligence, streamline crew and subcontractor management, and ensure high data quality within the construction management process.

## System Architecture
The platform utilizes a modern web stack for robustness and comprehensive functionality, adhering to a client-server architecture with a strong emphasis on security, scalability, and user experience.

-   **Frontend**: React with TypeScript, Wouter for routing, TanStack Query for data management, styled with Tailwind CSS and shadcn/ui.
-   **Backend**: Express.js in TypeScript, secured with CORS, rate limiting, and JWT token versioning.
-   **Database**: PostgreSQL on Neon, managed with Drizzle ORM, supporting various modules including audit logs, billing, time clock, AI operations, and project management. **Bid-Estimate-Project Integration**: Estimates can attach to bids or projects via nullable bidId/projectId columns with CHECK constraint enforcing at least one reference. During bid-to-project conversion, bidId retained for traceability while projectId added. Scope items linked to estimate line items via one-to-one UNIQUE constraint for automated quantity sync. **Quote Transfer System**: During bid-to-project conversion, subcontractor quotes automatically transfer as subcontractor contracts (with lead times preserved in payment terms and notes) and material quotes transfer as procurement items (with supplier info and lead times preserved for scheduling). Auto-creates subcontractor records if not existing, generates unique contract numbers (SC-{PROJECT_CODE}-{NUMBER}), and sets initial status to PENDING for manager review.
-   **Authentication**: JWT-based authentication using httpOnly cookies, bcrypt for password hashing, and token versioning, featuring Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) across six user roles and self-service registration.
-   **Security**: Multi-layered protection including rate limiting, comprehensive audit logging, and file validation.
-   **UI/UX**: Clean, functional interface built with shadcn/ui components and Tailwind CSS with a focus on intuitive project management workflows.
-   **Key Features**:
    -   **Authentication & Authorization**: Secure JWT with RBAC for granular access control.
    -   **User Management**: Admin tools for managing accounts, roles, and user activation.
    -   **Time-Clock System**: Native time-clock with GPS, shift management, and approval workflows.
    -   **Project Labor Hours**: Production-ready feature linking time-clock shifts to specific projects for profitability analysis. Displays employee-specific labor hours, hourly rates, and calculated labor costs per shift. Features server-side filtering (date range, approval status, employee) and sorting (8 options: date, hours, cost, employee name - ascending/descending), plus quick time period presets (Today, Yesterday, This Week, Last Week, Last 7/30 Days, This Month, Last Month, All Time) with timezone-safe date calculations. Includes aggregate metrics (total shifts, hours, cost, average rate) and proper null handling for active shifts. RBAC-restricted to managers, superintendents, and admins. Enables AI CFO to calculate true project profitability using actual labor costs.
    -   **Audit & Compliance**: Comprehensive audit logging for all critical actions.
    -   **Project Lifecycle Management**: CRUD operations for Bids, Projects, Estimates, Procurement, and Change Orders, including a simplified Change-Order State Machine and attachment management with file uploads (images, PDFs, CAD, Office docs) and RBAC. Support for change order deductions and adjusted contract calculations. **Simplified Change Order Workflow**: DRAFT → SUBMITTED (records submittedAt timestamp) → APPROVED/REJECTED. The submittedAt field tracks when a CO was sent to the client for response. Removed unnecessary PENDING_APPROVAL step for faster processing. Weekly automated alerts (Monday 8:00 AM) notify managers of stale change orders (>7 days without client response).
    -   **Daily Notes & Company Communications**: System for project-specific and company-wide daily communications with filtering, inline editing, and AI-powered analysis for actionable task generation, smart routing of insights, and severity levels.
    -   **Daily Operations**: Media uploads and daily scope progress tracking with quantity completion and billing status management. **Scope & Progress Interface**: Individual SOW item cards displaying per-item metrics (total, completed, billed, remaining quantities with units), visual progress bars for completion and billing percentages, and color-coded billing status badges. Cards are filterable by search term, unit type, and billing status.
    -   **AI Senior PM + CFO System**: AI-driven task generation, profitability analysis, scheduling, ordering, bid tracking, and billing automation. **Lead Time Intelligence**: AI analyzes material procurement items and subcontractor contracts (created from bid quotes) to detect scheduling conflicts, identify ordering deadlines based on supplier lead times, flag resource contention when multiple projects need same subcontractor, and generate proactive alerts when lead times conflict with project phasing schedules.
    -   **Takeoff + Estimating Engine**: Comprehensive system for managing project plans, interactive drawing tools (Linear, Area, Volume, Count, Arc/Radius), measurement persistence, quantity summaries, and multi-page PDF rendering. **Phase 2 Complete (Production-Ready):** (1) 5px click vs. drag threshold with Stage drag lifecycle tracking to prevent accidental measurements during pan operations, (2) Advanced pan/zoom with hand tool, spacebar temporary pan (hold to pan, release to restore tool), and cursor-centered mouse wheel zoom with synchronized Stage position during drag to prevent snap-back, (3) Arc tool with 3-point workflow (start, middle, end) where backend computes arc length via geometry utilities and stores in `computedLengthFt` for accurate curved measurements, (4) Comprehensive keyboard shortcuts (L/A/V/C/N/R/S/H, Shift+C, Delete, Esc, ?) with smart guards for text inputs, Radix UI portals, iframes, and multi-point drawing workflows. **Known Limitation:** Konva canvas does not respond to Playwright synthetic pointer events, requiring manual testing for canvas interactions.
    -   **File Storage & Permissions**: Management via Replit Object Storage with presigned URLs and granular ACLs.
    -   **Billing Status Workflow**: Production-grade workflow for managing billing lifecycle from field recording to invoicing, with strict RBAC and audit trails.
    -   **Project Financials Dashboard**: Real-time financial tracking on project overview displaying Budget (direct cost), Contract Amount (budget + 10% overhead + 15% profit), Adjusted Contract (with approved change orders), Projected Profit (expected margin), and Actual Profit (contract minus actual costs). Actual costs aggregated from time clock labor hours (at employee cost rates), procurement materials, subcontractor payments, and expense receipts. Includes profit variance tracking with color-coded status indicators. API endpoint: GET /api/projects/:code/financials.
    -   **Project Labor Rates**: Production-ready feature for configuring project-specific labor rates and overhead costs. **Rate Hierarchy**: Employee-project override > Project default > User default rate > System default ($50 regular / $75 OT). **Burden Multiplier**: Applied to both regular and overtime hours to account for payroll taxes, insurance, workers comp, and benefits (configurable 1.0x-1.5x). **API Endpoints**: GET/POST/PUT/DELETE at `/api/projects/:code/labor-rates` for project defaults, and POST/DELETE at `/api/projects/:code/labor-rates/employees` for employee overrides. **UI**: Dedicated Labor Rates tab in project detail showing current configuration, employee overrides table, and labor cost impact metrics. RBAC-restricted to ADMIN and MANAGER roles. Financial calculations automatically use the rate hierarchy and burden multiplier when computing labor costs.
    -   **Estimator Handoff Packet**: Comprehensive 7-section job information form for QuickBooks integration. **Sections**: (1) Job Overview (GC contact, site access), (2) Estimate Snapshot (contract value, bid/award dates, markup, duration, manpower, alternates), (3) Contract & Documentation (subcontract executed, insurance certs, bond, permits, retention, payment terms, SOV ready, draw schedule, prevailing wage), (4) Materials & Supply Chain (concrete supplier, mix info, rebar vendor, forming materials, curing specs, special items, lead time risks, submittal status), (5) Field Work Details (layout/survey by, benchmark info, access zones, work hours, safety orientation, inspection needs), (6) Project Notes (coordination issues, weather risks, soil conditions, estimator notes), (7) Communication Protocol (GC contact method, PM, superintendent, office admin, safety contact). **Data Flow**: Created/edited on bids, automatically transferred to projects during bid-to-project conversion with awardDate stamped. **RBAC**: GET restricted to management roles (ADMIN, MANAGER, ESTIMATOR, OFFICE_MANAGER, SUPERINTENDENT); PUT restricted to editing roles. **API Endpoints**: GET/PUT at `/api/bids/:code/handoff-packet` and `/api/projects/:code/handoff-packet`. **Component**: Reusable `JobInformationForm.tsx` with collapsible sections and inline editing.
