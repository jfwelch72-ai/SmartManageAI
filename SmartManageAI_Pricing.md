## Feature Details

### AI Project Manager (AI PM Pack)
Automated scheduling and task management powered by AI. Generates tasks, provides risk alerts, and delivers daily project summaries.

### AI CFO (AI CFO Pack)
Financial analysis and profitability insights. Tracks margins, forecasts cashflow, and sends financial alerts to keep your business healthy.

### AI Email (AI Email & Admin Pack)
Smart email classification and task extraction. Automatically categorizes emails across 15 categories, extracts actionable tasks, and manages follow-ups.

### AI Vision Takeoff (AI Vision Pack)
AI-powered plan analysis and takeoff using OpenAI GPT-4o Vision. Analyzes construction documents and extracts material quantities automatically.

### Website Builder (Website + SEO Pack)
White-label website with SEO tools. Includes page builder, blog, project gallery, social media integration, and analytics.

### Bid Network (Bid Network Pack)
Subcontractor bid solicitation network. Post bid requests to platform-member subcontractors with trade matching, invitation system, and response workflow.

### Production Rate Tracking (Growth+ plans)
Crew productivity and cost analytics. Tracks labor cost per UOM, material cost per UOM, and profitability metrics.

### Material Budget Tracking (Growth+ plans)
Budget variance alerts and tracking. Compare estimated vs actual material costs with real-time alerts.

### Vendor Invites (Growth+ plans)
Invite vendors to submit pricing directly through the platform.

### Advanced Reports (Growth+ plans)
Detailed project and financial reporting with customizable dashboards.

### Portfolio Dashboards (Scale+ plans)
Cross-project performance insights for managing multiple projects simultaneously.

### Multi-Organization (Hustler plan)
Portfolio management across multiple organizations with unified analytics.

### Priority Support (Scale+ plans)
Faster response times and dedicated support team access.
