🏗️ 1. Pre-Construction (Before breaking ground)
This is where good projects are made… or quietly doomed.
Key things:
•	Contract Agreement finalized 
•	Issued for Construction (IFC) Drawings 
•	Permits & licenses (building permit, safety permits, etc.) 
•	Project schedule (baseline timeline) 
•	Budget / cost breakdown (BOQ) 
•	Procurement plan (materials, suppliers, lead times) 
•	Site mobilization plan 
👉 If this phase is weak, expect delays, change orders, and headaches later.
________________________________________
📋 2. Project Planning & Setup
Turning documents into an actual executable plan.
Needed:
•	Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) 
•	Detailed construction schedule (lookahead plans) 
•	Resource planning 
o	Labor (workers, foremen) 
o	Equipment (cranes, mixers, etc.) 
•	Method statements (how each task will be done) 
•	Risk assessment + mitigation plan 
•	Safety plan (HSE plan) 
________________________________________
🚧 3. Site Setup & Mobilization
Making the site functional.
Includes:
•	Site office setup 
•	Temporary facilities (toilets, storage, barracks) 
•	Utilities (water, electricity) 
•	Site fencing & signage 
•	Equipment delivery 
•	Workforce deployment 
________________________________________
👷 4. Construction Execution (Core Phase)
This is where the actual building happens.
Daily essentials:
•	Manpower management 
•	Material delivery & tracking 
•	Equipment usage 
•	Daily work reports (DWR) 
•	Site supervision 
•	Progress tracking vs schedule 
Major activities:
•	Earthworks 
•	Structural works 
•	Architectural works 
•	MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing) 
________________________________________
🧾 5. Procurement & Materials Management
Because construction = logistics + timing.
Needed:
•	Purchase orders 
•	Supplier coordination 
•	Delivery schedules 
•	Inventory tracking 
•	Material inspection upon arrival 
👉 Late materials = idle workers = burning money 🔥
________________________________________
📊 6. Cost Control & Billing
Keep the project profitable (or at least not painful).
Includes:
•	Budget tracking 
•	Cost vs actual monitoring 
•	Progress billing (monthly) 
•	Subcontractor payments 
•	Change orders / variation orders 
________________________________________
🔄 7. Change Management (Very Real Life 😅)
No project goes exactly as planned.
Needed:
•	RFI (Request for Information) 
•	Change order tracking 
•	Client approvals 
•	Updated drawings / revisions 
________________________________________
🛡️ 8. Quality Control (QA/QC)
Making sure you don’t build something that falls apart… or gets rejected.
Includes:
•	Inspection checklists 
•	Material testing 
•	Work inspections 
•	Punch lists 
•	Compliance with specs & standards 
________________________________________
⚠️ 9. Safety Management (HSE)
Non-negotiable in real construction.
Needed:
•	Safety officer 
•	Toolbox meetings (daily briefings) 
•	PPE enforcement 
•	Incident reporting 
•	Safety audits 
________________________________________
📈 10. Progress Monitoring & Reporting
Keeping everyone aligned (client, PM, engineers).
Includes:
•	Daily reports 
•	Weekly progress reports 
•	Monthly reports 
•	Photos & documentation 
•	Schedule updates 
________________________________________
🤝 11. Subcontractor Management
You don’t build everything yourself.
Needed:
•	Scope definition per subcontractor 
•	Contracts & agreements 
•	Performance tracking 
•	Billing & payments 
•	Coordination between trades 
________________________________________
🏁 12. Project Closeout
Finishing strong (or scrambling last minute 😄)
Includes:
•	Punch list completion 
•	Final inspections 
•	As-built drawings 
•	Testing & commissioning 
•	Turnover documents 
•	Final billing 
________________________________________
💡 Real Talk (What Actually Makes or Breaks a Project)
From real-world experience:
•	Communication > Everything 
•	Planning saves money 
•	Bad procurement kills schedules 
•	Field workers need simple systems (not complicated apps) 
•	Change orders are guaranteed—just manage them well
