📊 In Bids (Preconstruction)
Plans & Takeoff are used to:
•	Win the job 
•	Build estimates 
•	Compare subcontractor quotes 
👉 This is:
“How much do we think this will cost?”
________________________________________
🏗️ In Projects (Post-Award)
Plans & Takeoff are used to:
•	Control actual costs 
•	Track materials + quantities 
•	Manage changes (change orders) 
•	Build Schedule of Values (SOV) 
👉 This is:
“How are we actually executing this?”
________________________________________
🔁 The correct system behavior (this is where most apps fail)
Do NOT create totally separate takeoffs.
Instead:
✅ Use a carry-over + versioning system
[BID]
Plans v1
Takeoff v1
Estimate v1
        ↓ (Project Awarded)
[PROJECT]
Duplicate → Takeoff v2
           (editable, more detailed)
           + linked to budget + SOV
________________________________________
🔥 Why this matters (huge advantage for your app)
If you separate them completely:
•	❌ Users redo everything manually 
•	❌ Data gets inconsistent 
•	❌ Wasted time 
If you connect them:
•	✅ 1-click conversion from bid → project 
•	✅ Historical tracking (what changed?) 
•	✅ Faster workflows 
•	✅ Much stronger cost control 
________________________________________

Takeoff Accuracy	Fast / Approx	Detailed / Final
