# SmartManageAI - Complete Platform Feature & Integration Guide

## Platform Overview

SmartManageAI is an enterprise-grade, white-label SaaS platform purpose-built for construction companies of every size and trade. It centralizes the entire construction project lifecycle into one intelligent system — from winning bids to final closeout — while providing AI-driven insights, real-time collaboration, and trade-specific customization for over 230 construction trades across 17 categories. The platform is multi-tenant by design, meaning each organization gets a fully isolated, brandable environment with its own users, data, and configuration.

## 1. AI-Powered Intelligence Suite

SmartManageAI embeds artificial intelligence into every layer of the platform, powered by OpenAI's GPT-4o and GPT-4o Vision models. These are not add-on gimmicks — they are deeply integrated tools that learn from your data and produce actionable results.

### AI Project Manager (AI PM)
The AI Project Manager acts as a virtual superintendent, continuously analyzing every active project to surface risks, generate tasks, and recommend actions before problems escalate.
- Automatically scans project data including budgets, labor hours, material consumption, schedule progress, and weather forecasts.
- Generates prioritized task lists with urgency ratings (Critical, High, Medium, Low).
- Produces risk alerts when budget overruns, schedule delays, or safety concerns are detected.
- Runs on-demand or on a scheduled cadence, delivering insights directly to the dashboard.
- Maintains a full history of AI-generated analyses for comparison and accountability.

### AI CFO & Financial Analyst
A dedicated financial intelligence engine that gives owners and executives instant visibility into the financial health of every project and the business overall.
- Calculates project profitability using real-time data from labor, materials, subcontracts, and change orders.
- Identifies cost variances and flags projects where spending is trending above estimates.
- Provides cash flow forecasting based on Schedule of Values billing, accounts receivable, and payable commitments.
- Generates executive-level financial summaries with plain-language explanations of complex financial data.

### AI Auto-Takeoff (Computer Vision)
One of the platform's most powerful features, the AI Auto-Takeoff uses computer vision to analyze uploaded construction blueprints and automatically extract material quantities.
- Accepts PDF plans and high-resolution images as input.
- Uses GPT-4o Vision to identify and measure linear distances, areas, and item counts directly from the drawings.
- Results are displayed on the interactive canvas for review, adjustment, and approval.
- Dramatically reduces the time spent on manual quantity takeoffs from hours to minutes.

### AI Scheduling Assistant
Takes the guesswork out of project scheduling by generating optimized timelines based on your actual project data.
- Analyzes scope of work items, historical production rates, and crew availability to propose schedules.
- Factors in weather forecasts and identifies potential weather-related delays.
- Highlights critical path tasks and scheduling conflicts.
- Produces interactive Gantt charts that can be manually adjusted after AI generation.

### AI Bid & Contract Analysis
Helps estimators and project managers quickly understand the requirements and risks in complex bid packages and contracts.
- Extracts key information from bid documents including dates, scope requirements, and special conditions.
- Analyzes contracts for liability clauses, payment terms, insurance requirements, and risk flags.
- Highlights unusual or potentially problematic contract language.
- Summarizes multi-page documents into actionable bullet points.

### AI Email Assistant & Classifier
Turns your inbox into a managed workflow by automatically understanding and categorizing incoming communications.
- Classifies emails into categories: RFI, Change Order Request, Billing Question, Delay Notice, Technical Question, and more.
- Extracts actionable items and can automatically create tasks from email content.
- Generates draft replies with selectable tone (Professional, Casual, Firm).
- Provides daily and weekly email digest summaries.

### AI Change Order Generator
Streamlines the change order process by automatically drafting professional change order documents.
- Input a description of the scope change, and the AI generates a complete change order document with justification, impact assessment, and cost implications.
- Pulls relevant project data to support the change order narrative.
- Produces documents ready for review and client presentation.

### AI Jobsite Camera Analysis
Brings computer vision to the field by analyzing jobsite photographs for progress, quality, and safety observations.
- Upload site photos and receive AI-generated observations about construction progress, material staging, and work quality.
- Identifies potential safety concerns visible in site imagery.
- Helps document daily progress with AI-written summaries tied to photographic evidence.

### AI Website Wizard
A guided AI assistant that helps construction companies build professional, SEO-optimized websites (see Website Builder section for full details).

### AI Scope Prediction
Predicts the scope of work items a project will need based on trade type, project characteristics, and historical data from similar projects, accelerating the estimating process.

### AI Vendor Pricing Extraction
Automatically extracts pricing data from vendor PDF catalogs, Excel spreadsheets, and price lists, populating your pricebook without manual data entry.

### AI Onboarding & Trade Discovery
A wizard that researches specific construction trades to pre-configure the platform with industry-standard scope items, materials, formulas, and calculators, so new users are productive from day one.

## 3. Estimating & Takeoff Engine

### Interactive Takeoff Workspace
A professional-grade 2D takeoff workspace built on an interactive canvas for precise quantity measurement from construction drawings.
- **PDF Blueprint Rendering:** Renders multi-page PDF plan sets directly in the browser. Supports sheet navigation, renaming, duplication, and reordering.
- **Sheet Folder Organization:** Organize takeoff sheets into collapsible folder groups (Plans, Supporting Documents) with drag-and-drop reordering between groups.
- **3-Panel Layout:** Left sidebar with document tree and sheet organization, center canvas for drawing and measurement, and right panel for measurement properties and aggregation.
- **Drawing & Measurement Tools:**
  - **Select Tool (S):** Select and edit existing measurements.
  - **Pan Tool (H):** Navigate around the drawing canvas.
  - **Zoom Tool:** Zoom in/out with scroll wheel, keyboard shortcuts (+/-), or button controls.
  - **Linear Tool (L):** Measure straight-line distances between two points.
  - **Arc/Radius Tool (R):** Measure curved surfaces and arc segments.
  - **Area Tool (A):** Draw polygons to calculate enclosed areas.
  - **Volume Tool (V):** Calculate volumes from area measurements with depth input.
  - **Count Tool (C):** Place markers to count individual items (fixtures, outlets, etc.).
  - **Note Tool (N):** Pin notes and annotations directly on the drawing.
  - **Linear + Drop Tool (D):** Measure linear distances with a vertical drop component (horizontal + vertical distances combined).
  - **Pitched Linear Tool (P):** Measure along pitched/sloped surfaces with automatic pitch adjustment factor.
  - **Pitched Area Tool (T):** Calculate area on pitched surfaces (e.g., roofing) with pitch ratio multiplier.
  - **Surface Area Tool (G):** Compute 3D surface area from flat measurements using pitch conversion.
- **Calibration System:** Manual calibration by drawing a known distance, plus preset architectural, civil, and structural scales. Includes a verification mode to confirm calibration accuracy.
- **Advanced Drawing Features:**
  - Snap-to-point functionality for precise measurements.
  - Ortho-lock (Shift key) for straight horizontal/vertical lines.
  - Undo/Redo with Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y keyboard shortcuts.
  - Full keyboard shortcut suite with help dialog (press ? to view).
  - Real-time measurement display during drawing.
- **Addenda Overlays:** Toggle visibility of addenda overlays to compare plan revisions directly on the canvas.
- **Revision Comparison:** Side-by-side comparison of document revisions with metadata tracking.

### Per-Measurement Cost Linking
Each individual measurement can be linked to cost data for real-time financial visibility during the takeoff process.
- Assign cost codes directly to individual measurements.
- Enter unit costs per measurement for instant estimated totals.
- View calculated totals (quantity x unit cost) in the measurement properties panel.
- Pitch ratio and computed values are stored and displayed for all measurement types.

### Quantity Categories
Organize measurements into logical groupings that bridge the gap between takeoff quantities and estimate line items.
- Create custom categories (Concrete, Framing, Roofing, etc.) with color coding.
- Assign measurements to categories during or after the takeoff process.
- Category totals aggregate automatically across all measurements.
- Categories feed directly into the estimating engine.

### Assembly Templates
Pre-built templates that auto-calculate multiple material and labor requirements from a single takeoff measurement.
- Define assemblies with multiple components, each with its own formula.
- Custom formula engine supports variables like length, area, volume, and count.
- Apply waste factors per component (e.g., 10% waste on drywall).
- Example: A "Stud Wall" assembly calculates studs, plates, drywall, tape, mud, and paint from one linear measurement.

### Estimating Engine
A hierarchical line-item estimating system for complete project cost analysis.
- Create detailed estimates with line items organized by CSI division, trade, or custom groupings.
- Track five cost categories per line item: Material, Labor, Equipment, Consumables, and Subcontract.
- Automatic markup and profit calculations with configurable percentages.
- Estimate versioning with Draft, Pending Review, and Approved statuses.
- Import quantities directly from the takeoff workspace.
- Hierarchical line item builder for complex estimate structures.
- Generate professional PDF quotes and bid summaries.

### Cost Codes & Labor Rates
- Comprehensive cost code system aligned with accounting standards.
- Trade-specific labor rate management with production rate benchmarks.
- Equipment rate tracking for owned and rental assets.

## 15. Security & Enterprise Features

### Authentication
- **JWT-Based Authentication:** Secure JSON Web Tokens stored in httpOnly cookies, protecting against XSS attacks.
- **Password Security:** Bcrypt hashing with configurable complexity requirements.
- **OAuth Social Login:** Sign in with Google or Apple for streamlined access.
- **CSRF Protection:** Cross-Site Request Forgery prevention on all authenticated endpoints.
- **Email Verification:** Required email verification for new account registration.

### SSO / SAML 2.0
Enterprise-grade Single Sign-On for large organizations.
- Support for major Identity Providers: Okta, Azure AD, OneLogin, and any SAML 2.0 compliant IdP.
- Metadata URL support for automatic configuration.
- Certificate validation for secure assertion verification.
- "Enforce SSO" mode to disable standard password login for maximum security.

### Session Security
Advanced session management and control.
- **Token Versioning:** Changing passwords or administrative revocation immediately invalidates all active sessions globally.
- **Configurable Idle Timeout:** Default 8-hour session expiry with organizational override.
- **Concurrent Session Limits:** Maximum of 5 simultaneous sessions per user (configurable).
- **Session Revocation:** Administrators can revoke all sessions for any user instantly.

### Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
A sophisticated hierarchical permission system with 18 distinct roles.
- **Platform Level:** SUPER_ADMIN for platform-wide management.
- **Organization Level:** ADMIN, MANAGER, OFFICE_MANAGER for organizational leadership.
- **Operations Level:** SUPERINTENDENT, FOREMAN, PROJECT_MANAGER for project operations.
- **Specialty Roles:** ESTIMATOR, ACCOUNTANT, SAFETY_OFFICER for functional expertise.
- **Field Roles:** FIELD_ENGINEER, HOURLY_EMPLOYEE for field workers.
- **External Roles:** VENDOR, CLIENT for portal access.
- **Enterprise Roles:** HUSTLER for multi-organization management.
- **Triple-Layer Enforcement:**
  1. Route-level middleware blocking unauthorized API access.
  2. Feature-level gating based on subscription tier and feature flags.
  3. Data-level organization scoping on all database queries.

### Multi-Tenant Architecture
Complete data isolation between organizations.
- Every database query is scoped to the requesting user's organization.
- Cross-tenant data access is physically prevented at the query level.
- Organization-specific configuration, branding, and feature toggles.

### The Hustler Role (Multi-Organization Management)
A unique enterprise feature for business owners who manage multiple companies.
- Single login to manage multiple organizations.
- Unified inbox and dashboard spanning all managed organizations.
- Delegation mode for assuming specific roles within sub-organizations.
- Cross-organization analytics and benchmarking.

### Rate Limiting
Protection against abuse and automated attacks.
- Sensitive endpoint protection (authentication, billing) with rate limiting.
- Brute-force prevention on login endpoints.
- API rate limits per user and organization.

### Feature Flags
Granular control over feature availability.
- Enable or disable specific modules per organization.
- Managed by SUPER_ADMIN for platform-wide control.
- Supports gradual feature rollouts and A/B testing.
