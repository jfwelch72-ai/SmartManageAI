# Acceptable Use Policy

| Owner | CTO |
| ----- | --- |
| Approver | CEO |
| Review cadence | Annual |

## 1. Purpose

Define what staff may and may not do with Company information assets,
systems, and accounts.

## 2. Scope

All employees, contractors, and interns; all Company-issued or
Company-paid assets; all Company accounts on third-party services.

## 3. Acceptable use

You **may**:
- Use Company systems and data for legitimate business purposes.
- Install software from approved sources, with current patches.
- Use approved personal devices for email/Slack only after they meet the
  endpoint baseline (see policy #12).

You **must**:
- Lock your screen when stepping away.
- Use the password manager and unique passwords; enable MFA wherever
  available.
- Report any suspected security event to security@ or the on-call IR
  channel.
- Acknowledge this policy on hire and on every material update.

## 4. Prohibited use

You **must not**:
- Use customer data outside what is required to perform your job.
- Share credentials or accounts.
- Disable security tooling (MDM, EDR, disk encryption, MFA).
- Connect production systems to personal cloud accounts or sync tools.
- Move customer data to non-approved systems (personal email, personal
  cloud, ad-hoc spreadsheets).
- Use the Service or Company systems to violate any law or third-party
  rights.

## 5. Privacy

The Company may, with reasonable cause and in accordance with law, monitor
the use of its systems. Personal use of Company systems is at your own
risk and may be subject to such monitoring.

## 6. AI tools

When using third-party AI assistants (e.g. coding copilots, chat
assistants):
- Do not paste customer data into a tool that has not been reviewed by
  the security team and added to the vendor inventory.
- Do not paste credentials, secrets, or proprietary source that is not
  yours to share.

## 7. Enforcement

Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including
termination. Egregious or repeated violations may be referred to law
enforcement.
