# Track compliance and expirations

One screen for COIs, W-9s, business licenses, OSHA training, and more.

## How to do it

1. Open /compliance.
1. Filter by *Type*, *Vendor*, or *Days until expiration*. The default view shows everything expiring in the next 60 days.
1. Click any row to see the document, the expiration date, and the contact for renewal.
1. Use *Send reminder* to email the vendor a self-service re-upload link.

## Tips

- Compliance categories are configurable per-org — add custom ones (e.g. *Bonding capacity letter*) under *Company Settings → Compliance categories*.
