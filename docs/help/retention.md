# Manage retention and releases

Track withheld retention by project and release it at substantial / final completion.

## How to do it

1. Open *Project → Financial → Retention*.
1. The dashboard shows withheld retention per pay app and per sub.
1. *Release retention* lets you release in part (e.g. at substantial completion) or in full (final).
1. Released retention flows to the next AIA billing automatically.
