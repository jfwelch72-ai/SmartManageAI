# Score teams with KPI templates

Standardized KPI templates per role, scored monthly.

## How to do it

1. Open /kpi.
1. Pick a template (Field Foreman, Project Manager, Estimator, Office Admin) — or build a custom one.
1. Each metric has a weight, a source (manual or auto-pulled from project data), and a target.
1. Run the *Monthly score* job to compute scores; managers review and adjust before publishing to the team.
