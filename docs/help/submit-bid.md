# Build and submit a bid

The 6-step bid workflow walks you from RFP capture to a sealed PDF proposal.

## How to do it

1. *Bids → New bid*. Attach the RFP PDF and the project plans.
1. Step 1 *Documents*: tag each upload as *RFP*, *Plans*, *Specifications*, or *Addenda* so AI parsing knows what to do.
1. Step 2 *Takeoff*: open the integrated takeoff workspace, set the page scale, and run the measurements you need (linear, area, count).
1. Step 3 *Estimate*: pick assemblies from the catalog. Quantities flow in from the takeoff automatically.
1. Step 4 *Vendors / RFQs*: send RFQs for any line item without a current price. Vendor responses populate the unit costs.
1. Step 5 *Markup*: apply OH&P, contingency, and bond rates. Live profitability updates per division.
1. Step 6 *Proposal*: pick a proposal template, review the rendered PDF, and click *Send* to email it to the owner.

## Tips

- Toggle *AI Estimator* in step 3 to draft a complete estimate from the takeoff — review every line before submitting.
- Versioned bids let you respond to an addendum without losing the original numbers — duplicate the bid and start from step 4.
