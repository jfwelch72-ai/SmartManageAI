# Sync vendor pricing catalogs

Keep unit costs current by importing vendor catalogs and tagging them by region.

## How to do it

1. Open /vendor-pricing → *Import catalog*.
1. Drop a CSV (template provided) or a vendor's Excel price list. Columns are auto-mapped; review the mapping before confirming.
1. Tag the catalog with *Vendor*, *Effective date*, and *Region*. Estimates pick the most recent catalog for the project's region automatically.
1. Re-import any time — old prices become *Historical* and stop populating new estimates.

## Tips

- Catalogs support unit conversions (LF → CY, EA → BX). Set the conversion factor once per item.
