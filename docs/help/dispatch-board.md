# Dispatch crews and equipment

A drag-and-drop board for tomorrow's crews, foremen, and shared equipment.

## How to do it

1. Open /dispatch-board.
1. Each row is a project; each column is the next 7 days.
1. Drag a crew (or piece of equipment) onto a project/day cell. Conflicts (over-assigned crew, equipment in the shop) are flagged in red.
1. Click *Publish* to push the dispatch to each crew's mobile home screen — they see their next-day assignment as soon as it's published.

## Tips

- Use *Templates* to repeat a typical crew layout from week to week.
