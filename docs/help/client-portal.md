# Share progress in the client portal

Give the owner a read-only view of schedule, photos, billings, and documents.

## How to do it

1. Open the project → *Client portal → Invite client*.
1. Add the client's email and pick which sections they should see (Schedule, Daily Reports, Photos, Billings, Documents). Defaults to all of the above except internal cost data.
1. Click *Send invite*. The client gets a magic-link email; no password required.
1. From the same page you can revoke or re-send the invite, and view a log of every page they viewed.

## Tips

- The portal lives at /portal — bookmark-friendly for the client.
- Use *Brand portal* under *Company Settings → White-label* to push your logo and colors into the portal so it feels like your site.
