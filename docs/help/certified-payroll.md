# Generate certified payroll reports

Produce DOL WH-347 and state-specific certified payroll reports.

## How to do it

1. Open /payroll → *Certified payroll → Generate*.
1. Pick the project and pay period. The system pulls approved time entries and matches each worker to their Davis-Bacon classification.
1. Review fringe benefit splits. Non-union shops can pay fringes as cash; union shops use the trust contributions.
1. *Generate* renders a WH-347 (and any state form your project requires). Sign electronically and submit.

## Tips

- Classification mappings (DOL → your roles) live under *Company Settings → Classifications* and only need to be set once.
