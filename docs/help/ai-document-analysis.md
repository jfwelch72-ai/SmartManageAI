# Let AI summarize a contract or bid

Turn a 200-page contract into a one-page summary with risk flags.

## How to do it

1. Open the document (contract, bid, or specification) inside any project.
1. Click *AI summarize*. Pick a profile: *Risk*, *Scope*, *Schedule*, or *Custom prompt*.
1. The summary appears alongside the document with each claim back-linked to the source page/paragraph.
1. Click any flagged risk to jump to the offending clause.

## Tips

- Use *Compare* on two versions of the same contract to see redlines plus an AI commentary on what changed and why it might matter.
