# Use the mobile app offline

The mobile app works fully offline and syncs in the background when reception returns.

## How to do it

1. Before heading to a no-signal site, open the app and tap *Sync now* on the home screen. This pre-downloads your assigned projects, current daily reports, and active RFIs.
1. While offline, file daily reports, take photos, fill out punch list items, clock in/out, and answer RFIs. Each action is queued locally.
1. When the device reconnects, the queue drains automatically. The home screen shows *Pending sync: N* until everything is uploaded.
1. If a conflict happens (e.g. someone edited the same RFI on the web), the app shows a side-by-side diff and you pick the winner.

## Tips

- Photos are queued at full resolution but uploaded in batches to conserve battery and data.
- View /mobile-sync on the web to see the same queue from a manager's perspective.
