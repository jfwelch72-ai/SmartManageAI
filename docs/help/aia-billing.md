# Send AIA progress billings

Generate G702/G703 PDFs against the SOV, get owner sign-off, and post to A/R.

## How to do it

1. *Project → Financial → AIA Billings → New billing*.
1. Pick the billing period (defaults to month-to-date).
1. For each SOV line, enter *% complete this period* or *Stored materials*. Retention applies automatically per line.
1. Click *Preview*. Review the rendered G702 / G703 — page numbers, totals, and stored-material columns are calculated.
1. Click *Send for owner signature*. The owner signs electronically; once signed, the billing is locked and posts to A/R.

## Tips

- If your owner requires the AIA Document logo, upload your AIA license under *Company Settings → Branding* and the watermark is added automatically.
- Toggle *Apply lien waivers* in the send dialog to require conditional / unconditional waivers from each sub before posting their pay app.
