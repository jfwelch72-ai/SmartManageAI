# Getting started with SmartManageAI

A 10-minute tour from a fresh login to a production-ready workspace.

## How to do it

1. Sign in at /login. Your first sign-in lands on /dashboard with the onboarding checklist open.
1. Open Company Settings → Profile. Set your legal name, EIN, default address, logo, and primary trade. The trade controls which dashboards and AI suggestions appear by default.
1. From the checklist, click *Invite teammates* and add at least one Project Manager and one Field user. Each gets an email invite and a temporary password.
1. Open *Projects* and create one sample project so the dashboard tiles populate with real data instead of empty states.
1. Visit *Integrations* and connect QuickBooks/Sage if you do accounting outside SmartManageAI — this enables the 3-way match and AIA billing exports.

## Role-based navigation

Every role gets its own sidebar tailored to their workflow. You only see the sections relevant to your job:

| Role | Sidebar groups |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | Home, Win Work, Projects, Workforce, Finance, Field Ops, Safety & Compliance, Marketing, Administration, Reports, Tools |
| **Manager** | Home, Win Work, Projects, Workforce, Finance, Field Ops, Safety & Compliance, Administration, Marketing, Tools |
| **Superintendent** | Today, Workforce, Safety & Compliance, Jobsite, Reference, Tools |
| **Estimator** | Home, Estimating, Reference, Projects, Field Ops, Compliance, Reports, Tools |
| **Field Worker** | Today, Jobsite, Safety, Communication, Reports, Tools |
| **Vendor** | Bid Inbox, Messages, Dashboard, Clients, Pricing, Purchase Orders, Schedule, Documents, Compliance, Analytics |

Several pages support direct URL tab links — e.g. `/safety?tab=training` opens the Safety Hub directly on the Training tab, and `/payroll-management?tab=certified-payroll` opens the Certified Payroll tab.

## Tips

- The onboarding checklist on /dashboard hides itself once every step is done. You can re-open it any time from *Help → Setup checklist*.
- If you don't see a feature you expect, check Company Settings → Feature flags. Some advanced modules (BIM, LiDAR, AI Estimator) are gated per-plan.
- Safety Training appears as a standalone sidebar item for Field Workers. Admins, Managers, and Superintendents access it through the Safety Hub (/safety → Training tab).

## Troubleshooting

- **I can't see Field users on the time-clock map** — confirm each user has a role of *Field* and a phone with location permission, then ask them to clock in once to register their device.
