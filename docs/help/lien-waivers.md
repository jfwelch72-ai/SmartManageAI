# Collect and store lien waivers

Conditional and unconditional waivers tied to each pay app and each sub.

## How to do it

1. When you send an AIA billing, toggle *Require lien waivers* to require subs to upload signed waivers before their portion of the pay app is paid.
1. Subs receive an email with the waiver template (your jurisdiction is auto-detected); they sign electronically.
1. Signed waivers appear under *Project → Financial → Lien waivers* and are attached to the corresponding pay app.
