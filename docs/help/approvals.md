# Approve work in your queue

Change orders, time entries, POs, vendor invoices, and lien waivers all funnel into one queue.

## How to do it

1. Open /approvals. The default view shows everything assigned to you.
1. Click an item to see the full record (the CO line items, the time entry geo-trail, the PO and matched invoice, etc.).
1. *Approve*, *Reject* (with reason), or *Request changes* (sends it back to the originator with notes).
1. Use the *Bulk approve* checkbox to clear straightforward items in one pass.

## Tips

- Approval chains (multi-step) are configurable in the no-code Workflow Builder.
