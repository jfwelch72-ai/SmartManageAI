# Connect QuickBooks, Sage, and more

Provider-agnostic Integrations Hub for accounting, email, calendar, telephony, and storage.

## How to do it

1. Open /integrations.
1. Click the provider tile (e.g. QuickBooks Online). Sign in via OAuth — credentials never leave the provider.
1. Map your accounts: AR, AP, COGS, Bank, and the cost-code ledger.
1. Pick sync direction: *Push* (SmartManageAI → provider), *Pull*, or *Two-way*.
1. Hit *Save*. The first sync runs immediately; subsequent syncs are scheduled (defaults to every 15 min).

## Tips

- Each integration is gated behind a feature flag so Super Admins can pilot it before opening it to all users.
