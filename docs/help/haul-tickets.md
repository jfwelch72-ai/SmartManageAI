# Log haul tickets and mass haul

Capture every load with truck #, material, weight/volume, and origin/destination.

## How to do it

1. Open /haul-tickets.
1. *New ticket* — pick truck, project, material, and unit (TON, CY, LD).
1. Quantity comes from the scale (manual entry or a connected scale via the Integrations module).
1. Sign with finger or stylus on the mobile app — both driver and operator signatures are stored on the ticket.
1. Reports roll up per project / per material / per haul direction for DOT submittals.

## Tips

- Calibrate scales periodically — *Equipment → Scales → Calibration*. Out-of-calibration scales block ticket creation.
