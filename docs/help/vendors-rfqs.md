# Send and track RFQs to vendors

Solicit quotes from multiple vendors at once and compare side-by-side.

## How to do it

1. Open the project → *Procurement → RFQs → New*.
1. Add line items (or pull from the estimate).
1. Pick vendors. They get an email with a one-click response link.
1. As responses come in, the comparison grid shows side-by-side unit prices, lead time, and total.
1. Click *Award* on a vendor to convert the RFQ to a PO automatically.
