# Manage surety bonds

Track bid, performance, and payment bonds against bonding capacity.

## How to do it

1. Open /surety-bonds.
1. Add bonds: type (Bid/Performance/Payment), surety, premium, principal amount, and expiration.
1. The capacity tile shows *Bonded backlog vs. aggregate capacity* so you can see remaining headroom at a glance.
1. Renewal alerts fire 60 days before expiration.
