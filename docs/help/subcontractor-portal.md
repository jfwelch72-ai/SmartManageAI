# Onboard subcontractors via the portal

Subs upload insurance, sign the master agreement, and accept work orders without a SmartManageAI login.

## How to do it

1. Open *Vendors → Subs → New sub* and add the company's email.
1. Click *Send onboarding link*. The sub lands at /portal and is walked through: company info → COI upload → W-9 upload → master subcontract signature.
1. Each upload is verified server-side (file type, page count, expiration date for COI).
1. Once complete, the sub appears as *Onboarded* in the Vendors table and can be assigned to subcontracts and POs.

## Tips

- If a sub's COI is about to expire, the compliance dashboard surfaces it 30 days out and they get a self-service re-upload link.
