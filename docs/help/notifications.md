# Tune your notification preferences

Per-channel (email, push, in-app, SMS) and per-event preferences.

## How to do it

1. Open /profile → *Notifications*.
1. For each event (RFI assigned, daily report submitted, time-clock approval, etc.), pick the channels you want.
1. Set quiet hours (e.g. 8 PM – 7 AM). Notifications during quiet hours batch into a digest.
1. *Test notification* sends a sample to confirm your settings work end-to-end.
