# Track equipment and maintenance

Asset register with utilization, maintenance schedule, and rental tracking.

## How to do it

1. Open /equipment.
1. Add a piece of equipment: type, serial, owned/rented, hour-meter or odometer, location.
1. Schedule preventive maintenance (every X hours / miles / days). Upcoming PMs appear on the maintenance dashboard.
1. Field users can log fuel, hours, and breakdowns from the mobile app — entries automatically debit the project they're assigned to.

## Tips

- Rental returns generate a reminder 7 days before the rental end-date so you don't get hit with extension fees.
