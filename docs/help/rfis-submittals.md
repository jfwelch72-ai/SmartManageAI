# Manage RFIs and submittals

Pipelines for both RFIs and submittals support external assignees and overdue alerts.

## How to do it

1. Open the project → *RFIs* (or *Submittals*) → *New*.
1. Pick a recipient. Internal users get the RFI in their queue; external recipients get an email with a one-click link (no login required).
1. Set *Required by* to enable overdue tracking. Two reminders go out before the due date and a daily reminder after.
1. Attach drawings, photos, or PDFs. Markups added in the plan viewer can be linked directly to the RFI.
1. Recipient answers in the portal. Their response is recorded in the audit trail and the RFI is marked *Closed*.

## Tips

- Convert an answered RFI into a Change Order in one click from the RFI detail page — line items pre-populate from the response.
- Submittal review chains support multi-stage approval (Architect → Engineer → Owner) with automatic forwarding.
