# Auto-generate a schedule with AI

Generate a CPM schedule from a project type, scope, and target completion date.

## How to do it

1. Open the project → *Schedule → AI generate*.
1. Pick a template (Commercial TI, Single-family, Highway, Bridge, etc.) and confirm the start date and key milestones.
1. The AI proposes tasks with durations and dependencies. Review the diff before applying.
1. Click *Apply* — tasks are created on your schedule, you take it from there.

## Tips

- AI durations come from your org's historical data when available; otherwise it falls back to industry medians.
