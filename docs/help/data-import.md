# Import data from CSV or other systems

Bulk-load projects, contacts, vendors, cost codes, and historical estimates.

## How to do it

1. Open *Company Settings → Data import*.
1. Pick the entity (Projects, Contacts, Vendors, Cost codes, Estimates).
1. Download the template CSV, fill it out, and upload. The mapping screen lets you confirm/override each column.
1. Click *Validate* to dry-run. Errors are reported per row; fix and re-upload before committing.

## Tips

- QuickBooks, Procore, and Sage exports have one-click presets — no manual mapping needed.
