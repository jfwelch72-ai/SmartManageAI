# Publish your white-label website

Spin up a marketing site at your own domain, fully branded, in under an hour.

## How to do it

1. Open /site.
1. Pick a template, drop in your logo, set primary/secondary brand colors. Live preview updates instantly.
1. Add pages: Home, Services, Projects, About, Contact (forms route to your CRM).
1. Connect a custom domain — *Settings → Domain* — and follow the DNS instructions. SSL is provisioned automatically.

## Tips

- Project case studies pull from your real projects (with photos and stats) once you mark them *Public*.
