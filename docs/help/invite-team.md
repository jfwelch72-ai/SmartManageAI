# Invite teammates and assign roles

Add new users, pick the right role, and (optionally) restrict them to specific projects.

## How to do it

1. Go to /company-settings → *Users*.
1. Click *Invite user*. Enter an email and pick a role: Admin, Project Manager, Field, Estimator, Accounting, or Subcontractor.
1. (Optional) Under *Project access*, switch from *All projects* to *Selected projects* to scope the invite — useful for subs you only want on one job.
1. Click *Send invite*. The user receives an email with a one-time link that expires in 72 hours.
1. Re-send or revoke the invite from the same Users table at any time.

## Tips

- Roles are additive: a user can be both *Field* and *Estimator*. Permissions are the union of both roles.
- *HUSTLER* and *SUPER_ADMIN* are internal roles for SmartManageAI staff and are not visible in the customer Users table.

## Troubleshooting

- **Invite never arrived** — ask the user to check spam, then re-send. If still missing, copy the link from the Users → ⋮ menu and share it directly.
