# Read the AI CFO insights

Daily-refreshed AI commentary on your portfolio's cashflow, margin, and risk.

## How to do it

1. Open /dashboard. The AI CFO card sits in the top-right.
1. It surfaces three things every day: *projects at margin risk*, *cashflow gaps in the next 60 days*, and *unusual cost variances vs. estimate*.
1. Click any insight to drill into the underlying data (the offending project, the variance breakdown).
1. Use *Snooze* to dismiss an insight for 7 days if it's a known/accepted situation.

## Tips

- The model only ingests aggregate KPIs from your org — it never sees customer or financial detail outside your tenant.
