# Upload material certifications

Capture cert sheets per delivery so DOT submittals stay airtight.

## How to do it

1. Open /material-certifications → *New certification*.
1. Enter material spec (DOT pay-item or your own catalog), supplier, lot number, and quantity.
1. Upload the cert PDF. The system OCRs the heat number and lot to make later searches easy.
1. Tie the certification to a project and (optionally) a specific haul ticket.
