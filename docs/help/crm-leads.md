# Capture leads with public forms

Embed lead-capture forms on your marketing site or share them as a public link.

## How to do it

1. Open /crm → *Forms → New form*.
1. Drag fields onto the canvas (text, email, phone, dropdown, file upload, signature).
1. Set the routing rules: which CRM owner gets the lead, which pipeline stage to drop it into, and what email to send the lead in confirmation.
1. Publish. You get an embed snippet and a public URL.

## Tips

- Forms support hCaptcha and honeypot fields automatically — no spam config needed.
